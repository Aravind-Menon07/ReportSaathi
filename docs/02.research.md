3. What AI Chatbots Already Do
Synthesizing evidence from studies on Large Language Models (LLMs), health literacy tools, and AI service terms:

Conversational Flexibility: General AI chatbots excel at processing natural language queries, translating complex medical definitions, and explaining generic clinical concepts in conversational prose.
Risk of Numerical Input Errors: Open-ended chatbots require users to manually type lab values or medicine names, creating a high risk of mistyping decimal points or units (e.g., entering 1.5 mg/dL as 15 mg/dL), which leads to dangerous misinterpretations.
Hallucination of Reference Ranges & Unsafe Drift: General LLMs frequently pull external or generic reference ranges when analyzing user-entered numbers, and open-ended system prompts easily drift into unauthorized medical diagnosing or altering drug dosages.
High Friction for Vulnerable Users: Text-heavy conversational interfaces present severe accessibility barriers for elderly, low-literacy, or non-English speaking patients who struggle to transcribe paper reports into typed prompts.
Data Privacy & PHI Retention Concerns: Unpaid AI API tiers and consumer chatbots allow human reviewers to read, annotate, and process user prompts and outputs for model training, creating severe data exposure risks for Protected Health Information (PHI) uploaded by patients.
Regulatory Exclusions in API Terms: Leading AI providers explicitly prohibit API use in clinical practice, for providing medical advice, or in any application overseen by medical device regulatory agencies (e.g., Gemini API Additional Terms of Service).
ReportSaathi's Strategic Advantage: ReportSaathi eliminates manual typing via automated OCR, anchors explanations strictly to the exact reference ranges printed on the user's uploaded document, enforces hard-coded safety guardrails against diagnosis/prescription, delivers instant regional language translations, and guarantees ephemeral zero-data-retention privacy compliance.

7. Decision
Decision: Build with conditions
One-line reason to switch: Patients switch to ReportSaathi because it instantly converts physical paper reports or lab PDFs into plain regional language anchored strictly to printed reference ranges—generating actionable doctor questions without consultation delays or the mistyping, hallucination, and privacy risks of general AI chatbots.
List of What is Still Unproven

OCR Extraction Accuracy: Ability to maintain $\ge 98\%$ accuracy in parsing low-quality mobile photos, blurry text, and handwritten doctor prescriptions without misreading decimal points or units (e.g., distinguishing 1.5 from 15).
Multilingual Clinical Nuance: Ability to translate complex medical terminology into Indian regional languages (Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, etc.) clearly without altering clinical meaning or sounding like informal medical advice.
Guardrail Robustness Under Adversarial Prompting: Consistency of system guardrails in preventing implicit diagnoses or medication adjustments when users actively ask for medical opinions or treatment changes.
Real-World Comprehension & Actionability: Proof that receiving plain-language summaries and generated questions measurably improves patient understanding, reduces anxiety, and enhances doctor-patient communication during consultations.
Trust vs. Over-Reliance Balance: User acceptance of ReportSaathi as an educational companion while strictly respecting boundaries and consulting qualified physicians for clinical decisions.
